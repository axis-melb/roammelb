📶 eSIM Basics 5 Q&As
An eSIM (embedded SIM) is a virtual SIM card built directly into your phone — no physical card needed. After purchase, you receive a QR code instantly by email. Scan it to activate. Instant delivery, instant setup.
A physical SIM requires visiting a store or airport, registering ID, and often queuing. An eSIM is purchased online, delivered instantly to your email, installed by scanning a QR code — no ID registration required. It also doesn't take up your physical SIM slot, so you can keep your Australian number active simultaneously.
RoamMelb sells Data-Only eSIMs — no phone number and no SMS function are included. No ID verification is required.

You can still use all data-based voice services: WhatsApp calls, WeChat voice/video calls, FaceTime, etc.

Common installation errors and fixes:
❌ Device does not support eSIM
Your phone may not have eSIM capability, or it may be a region-specific model that blocks eSIM (e.g. dual physical SIM models sold in mainland China). Check compatibility first — see "Does my phone support eSIM?"
❌ No internet connection during install
Downloading an eSIM profile requires a stable internet connection (Wi-Fi or existing mobile data). Make sure you are connected before attempting installation.
❌ QR code already used
Each eSIM QR code can typically only be scanned once. If it has already been installed on a device, the code is no longer valid for a second device. Contact us if this happens.
❌ Android error: "Could not activate service"
This error occasionally occurs on Android and may be caused by a corrupted profile or incomplete download. Try deleting the eSIM and reinstalling by scanning the QR code again. If the problem persists, contact support.
None of the above helped? Email contact@roammelb.com with your order number and a screenshot of the error. We reply within 2 business days.
